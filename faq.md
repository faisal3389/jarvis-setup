## Q1 — How do I export my notes?

Easy! Open **Settings → Export** and pick **Markdown** or **PDF**. You can export a single note,
a folder, or everything at once. The file opens in the iOS share sheet, so you can save it to
Files, AirDrop it, or send it to another app.

## Q2 — How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so cancelling happens in iOS, not inside the app:
open the iPhone **Settings app → tap your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Acme Notes → Cancel Subscription**.
You keep Pro until the end of the current period, and your notes stay yours either way.

## Q3 — I got a new phone and Pro is gone. How do I get it back?

No worries, nothing is lost. Make sure the new phone is signed in with the **same Apple ID**
you used to subscribe, then open Acme Notes and go to **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
Pro comes back in a few seconds.

## Q4 — How does sync work?

Acme Notes syncs through **iCloud**. As long as every device is signed in to the same Apple ID
and iCloud is turned on for Acme Notes (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud),
your notes show up everywhere, usually within a few seconds of an edit.

## Q5 — How much does Acme Notes Pro cost?

Two simple plans: **$4.99/month** or **$39.99/year**.
Both unlock everything in Pro. The free version stays free forever.

## Q6 — Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored on your device first, so you can read and write notes with no
connection at all. Changes sync to your other devices automatically when you're back online.

## Q7 — Is my data private?

Your notes stay in **your own iCloud** account. We don't run servers that store your notes,
we can't read them, and we never sell data. Basic, anonymous usage stats help us fix bugs.

## Q8 — Does Pro work with Family Sharing?

Yes! Both plans support Apple **Family Sharing**, so up to five family members can use Pro
on the same subscription. Each person's notes stay private in their own iCloud.

## Q9 — Can I get a refund?

Because all purchases go through Apple, **Apple processes refunds**, and we can't issue them
directly. Request one at **reportaproblem.apple.com**: sign in, find the Acme Notes purchase,
and choose "Request a refund". Apple usually replies within a couple of days.

## Q10 — How do I contact you, and how fast do you reply?

Just reply to any of our emails or write to **support@example.com**. It's a tiny company (mostly me!),
and we answer within **one business day**, usually much faster.
